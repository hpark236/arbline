Every upcoming NFL, MLB, NBA and NHL game, priced three ways: Polymarket, Kalshi, and the DraftKings line. Each number is what it costs, fees included, to own a claim that pays $1 if that team wins.

Buy the cheapest claim on each side. If the two add up to less than $1.000, the payout is locked whoever wins. Under 1.000 is an arbitrage; anything above it is the price of disagreement between venues.

How a price is built

Sportsbook: 1 / decimal odds of the DraftKings moneyline (via ESPN's public scoreboard). Polymarket: best ask on the CLOB plus the taker fee C × rate × p × (1−p), rate read from each market's fee schedule. Kalshi: best of YES on the team or NO on the opponent, plus 0.07 × p × (1−p).

What a sub-1.000 pair does not tell you

Sportsbook limits and account restrictions, a 1-cent-per-order Kalshi fee round-up, settlement timing, and rule mismatch (NFL ties push at the book; prediction markets resolve them by their own rules). Open a game to size the trade by real order-book depth.
